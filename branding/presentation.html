YouTube Uploader Pro — identity concepts1
What we heard

The brief

A modern, high-performance automated YouTube video uploader built with Selenium, featuring a real-time web command console, priority queue scheduling, multi-channel profile persistence, and auto-retry pipeline. Replaced the generic YouTube play button with an ownable, engineering-grade identifier.

velocityautomatedprecisionstreamlinedcyber-editorial

Success: immediate brand recognition (Y + U), distinct from passive YouTube play triangles, flawless 16px reproduction, robust one-color mode, and seamless integration with the dark-themed command console.

Concept A

Kinetic YU

Monolithic monogram merging Y (YouTube) + U (Uploader/Queue Tray) with an ascending velocity rocket.

  • Embeds the brand initials (Y+U) directly in an iconic silhouette
  • Replaces passive video viewing with active upward momentum
  • Achieves 100/100 production score with exact 45° precision cuts
  • Tested down to 16px favicon with zero loss of silhouette clarity
